How years owned, warranty years and miles are calculated

Years owned = earlier of years until age/ownership cutoff and years until optional odometer cutoff. Remaining warranty years = minimum of years owned, remaining time until original warranty-age cutoff, and remaining warranty odometer miles divided by miles driven per year. Warranty is capped at zero if either cutoff is already exceeded. It is never counted when the warranty does not transfer.

How interest, loans and payment-free time are calculated

Amount financed = purchase price + sales tax + fees − down payment. With a fixed loan length and monthly interest rate r = APR/12, the regular payment is P × r / (1 − (1+r)^(-N)), or P/N for 0% APR. With a fixed budget, that budget is paid monthly until payoff. We simulate interest and payments month by month; the final payment is reduced to the remaining balance. Time after payoff and before replacement is payment-free. A balance remaining at sale is settled then and is not counted as extra vehicle cost (principal is already in the purchase price).

Why resale matters, and what the estimate means

Net ownership cost = purchase price + purchase taxes + fees + interest paid or accrued through exit + annual running costs × owned years − estimated resale value. Dividing by owned years or miles gives net cost per year or mile. Down payments and loan principal are not added again. If resale is unknown, net cost is not reported; instead the before-resale spending metric remains available.

The estimated resale mode begins with today's entered purchase price and compounds an annual depreciation rate calculated from a published segment's historic five-year depreciation percentage. This is not a historical model for the individual car and is especially uncertain for 10–20-year-old vehicles. The low/high band uses 75% and 125% of the assumed annual rate as scenario stress tests; it is not a statistical confidence interval. A used-car's price today is the starting point; no original MSRP is inferred.

What this version doesn't model

Actual model-specific resale prices, mileage/condition adjustments to predicted resale, unexpected repairs, changing insurance or fuel prices, time value of money, sales tax on future sales, sales/trade-in transaction costs, loan fees, warranties with component-specific coverage, deductibles, inflation, and differences in accident risk. You can enter an annual running-cost estimate per vehicle, but these are assumptions, not forecasts.

Data is saved to local storage on this device. Export your scenario as JSON to move it to another browser. Nothing is sent to a server.
